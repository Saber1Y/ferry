Zcash · NU6.3 · ZIP 318

Move your ZEC from Orchard to Ironwood without showing anyone your balance.

Since NU6.3, Orchard funds can only be moved out. Move them in one transaction and the amount is public. FERRY moves them the private way, from your own computer: standard amounts, signed once, sent one at a time at random intervals.

How it works

  1. Connect your wallet

    Enter the recovery phrase of the wallet holding Orchard funds. FERRY keeps only a view-only copy; the phrase is wiped as soon as the wallet is set up.

  2. Review the plan

    Your balance is cut into the standard pieces ZIP 318 defines. You see every piece, the fees and the timeline before anything is signed.

  3. Sign once

    Enter the phrase again and FERRY signs every piece in one go, then wipes it. Nothing that can spend is ever written to disk.

  4. Let it cross

    FERRY sits in your menu bar and sends one piece at a time, a random while apart, through a different server than the one it syncs from. Leave your computer on.

What it hides, and what it does not

Strangers on the chain
See standard-size crossings at random times, the same shape as every other wallet following ZIP 318. Each crossing's amount is public by design.
Your computer
Holds a view-only key and sees your whole wallet history. That is why FERRY runs on your own machine, not on a server.
The servers
One sees your syncs, another your broadcasts, never in the same session. The demo build connects without Tor, so they can still see your IP address.
The crowd
Standard amounts hide you among the other wallets migrating at the same time. On a demo chain that crowd is small; on mainnet it is every ZIP 318 wallet.

Try it on the demo chain

  1. Download FERRY and open it.
  2. Click Get a demo wallet. You get a wallet holding fake Orchard coins on our demo chain.
  3. Restore it, click Plan the crossing, review, and sign.
  4. Watch the pieces land over the next half hour; the demo chain makes a block every 10 seconds instead of 75.

Evidence

Every claim on this page comes from recorded runs on a real Zcash node (Zebra), with transaction ids.

  • Run 1: first full migration accepted by a node; the leftover-balance bug found and fixed.
  • Run 2: 13 crossings in 13 blocks, beside Bob's one-shot migration.
  • Run 3: the same, driven by FERRY's own engine.
  • Run 4: a person migrating from the desktop app.
